import ChannelChart from '../../components/charts/ChannelChart';
import layers from '../../data/layers.json';
export const revenueLayer = layers[0];
export const retailers = revenueLayer.channels.filter(c => c.channel_type === 'retailer');
export const distributors = revenueLayer.channels.filter(c => c.channel_type === 'distributor');
export const dtc = revenueLayer.channels.filter(c => c.channel_type === 'DTC');

## {revenueLayer.label}

<p class="section-subtitle">{revenueLayer.subtitle}</p>

Revenue concentration is the first thing to notice. The top three channels — the national mass retailer ($3.6M/yr), the conventional grocer ($3.5M/yr), and the natural grocer ($3.3M/yr) — account for 41% of total revenue, but no single channel exceeds 15%. Revenue is more evenly distributed than it appears. Still, comparing Walmart to UNFI is misleading: one is a retailer, the other a distributor. The business relationship, cost structure, and deduction profile are fundamentally different. What follows separates channels by segment so like is measured against like.
